Read this before you nominate

The most important thing a nomination has to show is that the nominee made a significant contribution to local government beyond their own community.

Reviewers don't know your nominee. They decide using only the nomination form and the letters you include, so a reputation that's well known locally won't carry the nomination by itself. Put it on paper.

Who is eligible

  • Served as an elected official, paid professional, or appointed official of a city, town, public trust, or an organization that advances municipal government.
  • That service took place in Oklahoma.
  • Exemplifies public service with unquestioned ethics.

What strengthens a nomination

  • Two to four character reference letters
  • Awards or recognition from municipal governments or professional associations
  • Recognition from organizations outside their own local government or affiliate group
  • Membership in good standing in a related association
  • Active service in community or civic organizations
  • Years of service in Oklahoma
  • Anything else that sets the nominee apart from their peers

What to send

  1. The completed nomination form. Download the nomination form (PDF).
  2. Up to four reference letters or resolutions, one page each at most.
  3. The nominee's résumé or vita.
  4. Send everything to the Foundation Secretary by {{deadline}}: {{contactBlock}}
